FLOOD PREPAREDNESS

Purpose:
This document provides general preparedness information for communities facing flood risk. It is intended for educational and preparedness assistance and does not replace instructions from local authorities or emergency services.

BEFORE A FLOOD

Stay informed about weather and flood information from official government and emergency-management sources.

Prepare an emergency kit containing essential medicines, drinking water, food that does not require refrigeration, a flashlight, batteries or power bank, basic first-aid supplies, important documents, and essential personal items.

Keep important documents and valuables in waterproof containers or elevated locations where possible.

Charge mobile phones and backup power banks when severe weather or flooding is expected.

Identify safer locations and possible evacuation routes in advance. Follow evacuation instructions issued by local authorities.

If possible, move vehicles, electrical equipment, and other valuable items to higher locations before flooding begins.

DURING A FLOOD

Follow instructions and evacuation orders from local authorities and emergency services.

Avoid walking or driving through floodwater. Floodwater can hide hazards, have strong currents, and may be contaminated.

Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water. If instructed by authorities or qualified personnel, disconnect electricity safely.

Move to higher and safer ground when necessary.

Keep children and vulnerable people away from floodwater and dangerous areas.

Use official information sources for emergency instructions rather than relying on unverified social-media messages.

AFTER A FLOOD

Return home only when authorities indicate that it is safe to do so.

Avoid standing water because it may contain contaminants, debris, damaged electrical equipment, or other hazards.

Do not use electrical appliances that have been exposed to water until they have been inspected by a qualified professional.

Use safe drinking water. Follow local public-health guidance if water supplies may be contaminated.

Document damage where safe to do so and contact the appropriate authorities or service providers for assistance.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE

Āśraya provides information and risk-assessment support. It does not issue official evacuation orders or replace emergency authorities. In an immediate emergency, users should contact the appropriate local emergency services and follow official instructions.
